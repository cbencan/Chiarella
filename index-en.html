On the vendor side

I went from leading pre-sales to becoming a commercial director. I worked on hundreds of commercial projects across all industries and throughout Latin America; for years I traveled almost every week to a different country. I learned the pain points of every area of a business and the application solutions that address them: ERP, SCM, CX and HR. From the Innovation and Digital Transformation area we drove dozens of initiatives and pilots: 25 digital transformation and innovation projects and pilots. Here they are, grouped by area and in a public version.
Commercial and channel

Bringing the company closer to its customer

  • Ferre Go — digital channel for hardware stores and distributors.
  • Marketplace and channel data — platform and analytics to better understand the channel.
  • TuCasa — financing platform for home construction, through the channel.
  • Esquina de la Vida — structural assessment and reinforcement of homes vulnerable to earthquakes.
  • Camioncito — logistics simulation to serve distributor orders more efficiently.
  • Smart Pricing — analytical support for pricing policy.
Supply chain

More visibility and better decisions

  • Scrap sourcing platform — connecting recyclers and primary sources with the plant.
  • Price monitor — tracking and forecasting of raw material prices.
  • TMP / WMS — transportation and warehouse management platform.
  • S&OP and Supply Chain Planning — integrated sales and operations planning.
Plant and industry 4.0

Data and AI in operations

  • Scrap mix optimization — decision-support algorithm for the steel shop.
  • Ferroalloy dosing — model to optimize additions in the process.
  • Smart Factory — predictive maintenance and plant analytics.
  • Energy peak prediction — AI project (2021).
  • Computer vision — scrap classification by image.
  • Safety Analytics — analytics to strengthen safety.
Automation and data

Foundations to scale

  • Web scraping — automatic capture of market information.
